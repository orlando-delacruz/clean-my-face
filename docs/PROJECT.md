# Project Overview

CleanMyFace is a skincare / dermocosmetics brand by **Peerpharm**. This repository is the workspace for a **paid website test** (₱6,000) to design and build a homepage/landing page that translates the client's supplied brand brief into a polished, cohesive web experience. The test is coordinated through **John Frederick J. Dimas**, a Cosmetics Product Development Specialist, who is liaising with the client.

The primary confirmed deliverable is a **homepage / landing page**. A secondary page may also be expected, but this is **not yet confirmed**. The client is specifically interested in experience with beauty and wellness brands, so the work must demonstrate an appropriate understanding of skincare/dermocosmetics branding, UX, presentation, and content hierarchy.

This repository represents a paid evaluation/test project. The implementation is intended to demonstrate the proposed website direction and development capability. Production requirements, sitemap, functionality, and final content may change following client review and approval.

### Brand

Core messaging:

- **CLEAN WITH PURPOSE**
- **SKINCARE THAT GETS TO THE POINT**
- **Effective Formulas, Gentle on Skin, Approachable Skincare.**
- **Dermocosmetics made for real skin, real routines, real results.**

Four pillars:

1. Clear Job — Targeted Skin Needs
2. Purposeful Ingredients — Backed by Dermatology
3. Intentional Texture — Designed for Real Use
4. Right Format — The Best Way to Deliver Results

Supporting message: **"Different skin needs. Same gentle standard."**

## Purpose

- Produce a polished homepage/landing page that demonstrates the ability to transform a detailed cosmetics brand brief into a clear, premium website experience.
- Prove strong visual design, UX, content hierarchy, and product presentation for a beauty/wellness brand.
- Preserve the brand's positioning around purposeful, approachable skincare.

## Business Rules

Content rules:

- The supplied client brief is the **primary source of truth** for brand and product content.
- Do not invent product claims, pricing, ingredients, benefits, or other brand information.
- Do not silently modify conflicting product information; client clarification is required when supplied content is inconsistent.
- Homepage content should prioritize clarity and hierarchy over displaying every available detail.
- Product information should be organized by the appropriate range and routine, and the two ranges must remain clearly differentiated.

Project rules:

- Do not assume unconfirmed functionality; any ecommerce, purchasing, booking, consultation, or transactional workflow requires explicit client confirmation.
- Any functionality not explicitly required should not be assumed to be part of the project.
- The website should preserve the brand's positioning around purposeful, approachable skincare.

## Important Constraints

- Avoid inventing requirements, brand identity elements, product claims, or pricing/purchase flows.
- Do not treat proposed sitemap/pages as confirmed requirements.
- Preserve approved client content; flag inconsistencies instead of silently correcting them.
- Avoid unnecessary scope expansion; do not overengineer.
- Keep the homepage focused and understandable.
- The final experience should feel appropriate for a professional skincare/dermocosmetics brand.

- The two documented product-content inconsistencies below are the known unresolved product-copy issues; other product content is used as supplied in the brief.
- **Issue 1 — Plump It Up Step 02:** the product system names it **Milky Cushion Cleanser**, while the detailed product section refers to it as **GEL CLEANSER** (and the asset folder is named `GEL CLEANSER`). The official product name must be confirmed with the client.
- **Issue 2 — Stay Clear Step 02:** the product is **2% BHA Gel Cleanser**, but its supplied description appears to describe the preceding cleansing oil and appears to be duplicated content. The correct approved description must be confirmed with the client; no replacement copy should be invented.
- Final logo, typography, color palette, and other brand assets are not currently available in the inspected repository; their availability outside the repository is unconfirmed.
- Technical implementation, hosting/deployment, and responsive/breakpoint requirements were unconfirmed at the time of documentation.
- The supplied brand brief is not stored in the repository, so it must be referenced externally.
