Notes:

- Both photos are from the same photographer. The lab photo (scientist in a white
  coat and gloves handling a flask with test-tube racks) communicates formulation
  expertise and is used in the 4/5 portrait frame with a left-of-center focal point
  (`35% center`) to keep the scientist and flask in frame.
- The skincare photo (Asian woman in a white robe holding a skincare product in soft
  natural light) reinforces the Asian skincare inspiration and is used in the 1/1
  square frame with a right-of-center focal point (`68% center`) to keep the subject
  in frame.
- Neither photo shows readable third-party branding.
- Files were downloaded at 1920 px wide (Pexels `w=1920` originals). Original aspect
  ratio 3:2 preserved; the frames present the full image via `fit="contain"`
  (no cropping) at the 4/5 and 1/1 ratios. Derivatives are generated by
  `npm run build:images`.
- Superseded: the Phase 2 Moni Rathnak portraits (`expertise-model.jpg`,
  `expertise-professional.jpg`) were replaced by the specified Pexels sources above
  and their files, registry entries, and derivatives removed.
- The previous Expertise photos (`additional-img-1.jpg`, `additional-img-6.jpg`) remain
  registered in `src/data/images.ts` because `tests/registry.test.ts` asserts they
  resolve. They are no longer rendered.
